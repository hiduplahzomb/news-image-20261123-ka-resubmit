# Sources — 23 November 2026 world news

Every fact in `entry.png` was checked against reporting published on
23 November 2026. No numbers, names, or quotes were invented.

## 1. Lead — COP31 collapses (Antalya, Türkiye)
- UN climate talks ran ~48 hours into overtime and ended with no
  overarching agreement; ~200 countries failed to agree, after the EU
  and others refused any deal without fossil-fuel phase-out language.
  First full collapse of a COP's main political agreement since
  Copenhagen 2009.
- Sources: UBStandard (world-news brief, 23 Nov 2026); World News Wire
  morning edition 23 Nov 2026 (Xinhua); News Media House.

## 2. Major — Dymer strike kills 11 (Kyiv region, Ukraine)
- A Russian drone hit a five-storey apartment building in Dymer,
  ~45 km north of Kyiv, partially collapsing it. The death toll rose
  through Monday 23 Nov to 11 (regional governor Tymur Tkachenko,
  via Reuters), including a teenager; rescuers pulled a child out
  alive. A separate strike on a supermarket in Vyshneve killed 3.
  The card's headline and metric name the Dymer toll (11, Reuters);
  the earlier draft's "153 drones, ~60 jet-powered" mixed two
  different overnight waves and was removed.
- Sources: Reuters, 23 Nov 2026; Associated Press via World News
  Wire; Kyiv regional governor's Telegram via Razón y Saber, 23 Nov.

## 3. Major — Ukraine sinks Russian frigate Merkury (off Libya)
- Russian frigate Merkury sank off Benghazi after a suspected
  Ukrainian naval drone attack; President Zelenskyy claimed
  responsibility for the attack on Russian ships near Libya.
- Sources: Al Jazeera live blog 23 Nov 2026; SimpleLife.news daily
  news 23 Nov 2026.

## 4. Major — Democrats clinch 258 House seats (US)
- Democrats clinched at least 258 seats in the US House of
  Representatives, the Associated Press reported — the largest House
  majority for either party since 1992.
- Source: Associated Press via World News Wire, 23 Nov 2026.

## 5. Minor — Alcaraz wins ATP Finals (sport)
- Carlos Alcaraz beat world No. 1 Alexander Zverev 6-2, 7-6(7) to
  win his maiden ATP Finals title, completing an undefeated 5-0
  week in Turin. Date note: the final was played Sunday evening,
  22 Nov, in Turin (CET); it was the day's top sports story across
  Monday 23 Nov coverage (Reuters, The Times, BBC). It replaces the
  Korea $1T export story, whose milestone fell on 19 Nov, not 23 Nov.
- Sources: Reuters, 22–23 Nov 2026; The Times, 23 Nov 2026;
  tennistourcalendar.com final results, 23 Nov 2026.
